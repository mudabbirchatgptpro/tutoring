What one hour looks like

  • About 40 minutes one on one with me, on the skill your child is stuck on.
  • About 15 minutes on a practice engine I built. It picks each next question from what your child got right and wrong, and it moves on only when a skill holds.
  • 5 minutes with you: what we worked on and what comes next.

Between sessions, your child can do short practice sets on the engine at home. I see the results before the next session.

What I tutor

  • Math: fractions, percent, ratios, early algebra, geometry, word problems.
  • Reading and writing: vocabulary, spelling, reading for meaning, written answers.
  • ISEE practice for private-school admissions.
