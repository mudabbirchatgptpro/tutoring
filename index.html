How it works

  1. We meet you to understand what your child needs.
  2. We get to know your child and what they love.
  3. We design and teach every session to make learning fun.

What a session looks like

Say your child loves soccer. Their teacher builds a soccer game around this week's math, and your child scores only when the math is right. They build it, play it and fix it together, with the teacher guiding every step.

Grades 3 to 5

Reading, writing and number sense, made fun enough that kids ask for more.

Grades 6 to 8

Pre-algebra, stronger writing and getting ready for what comes next.

For educators and researchers

Qurios is a team of Harvard-graduate educators building on one design: a human teacher leads, and AI works for the teacher, not in place of the child's thinking. The design draws on four lines of work:

  • Hybrid human-AI tutoring, where tutors and software work together (Thomas, Aleven, Koedinger et al., LAK 2024)
  • AI support for the human tutor, from Stanford's Tutor CoPilot trial (Wang, Demszky et al., 2024)
  • Hints, not answers: open AI access hurt learning while guardrailed hints avoided most of the harm (Bastani et al., PNAS)
  • Learning by building things children care about (Papert, Resnick)
